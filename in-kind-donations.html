In-Kind Donations

Donate food, groceries, transport, equipment or services to support our mission.

What Can Be Donated

Many forms of generosity keep us going

Beyond financial gifts, in-kind contributions help us reach more people with less waste.

Food & Groceries

Non-perishable food, groceries and pantry staples for our distribution and community-kitchen programmes.

Fresh Produce

Surplus fruit, vegetables and other perishables from farms, markets and retailers, safely rescued and redirected.

Transport

Vehicles, fuel or logistics support to help us collect and deliver food efficiently and safely.

Equipment

Shelving, cold-storage units, packaging materials and other equipment that strengthens our operations.

Storage

Warehouse or storage space to help us safely hold and manage food and other donated goods.

Professional Services

Legal, accounting, marketing, IT or other professional skills donated to support our operations.

How Businesses Can Donate

Businesses of any size can partner with us to donate surplus stock, goods or services on a once-off or ongoing basis. We work with you to agree on what, when and how much you're able to give.

How To Arrange Delivery Or Collection

  • Complete the donation enquiry form below with details of what you'd like to donate.
  • Our team will get in touch to confirm quantities, timing and logistics.
  • We'll arrange collection, or agree on a convenient drop-off at our warehouse.
