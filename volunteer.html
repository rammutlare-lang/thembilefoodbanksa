Volunteer & Give In Kind

Time, food, goods, services and your voice: every kind of contribution keeps us going.

Volunteer With Us

Give your time and skills

Our four core activities all run on volunteer power. Choose where you'd like to help.

Food Redistribution

Help sort, pack and deliver rescued surplus food to schools, clinics and community centres.

Community Gardens

Support garden set-up days, seed-bank drives, and follow-up visits to household growers.

Nutrition Workshops

Assist facilitators at clinic and school nutrition sessions, or help prepare materials.

Emergency Response

Join our trained response network, ready to mobilise when disaster strikes.

Donate Food

Every food donor plays a part

Surplus, safe, quality food from farms, stores and kitchens can feed families instead of going to waste.

Farmers & Producers

Surplus or cosmetically imperfect harvest that would otherwise go to waste on the farm.

Supermarkets & Retailers

Near-date, overstocked or unsold but still safe food from stores and distribution centres.

Households

Unopened, non-perishable groceries that members of the public would like to donate directly.

In-Kind Donations

Many forms of generosity keep us going

Beyond food and funds, these contributions help us reach more people with less waste.

Transport

Vehicles, fuel or logistics support to help us collect and deliver food efficiently.

Equipment & Storage

Shelving, cold-storage units, packaging materials and warehouse space.

Professional Services

Legal, accounting, marketing, IT or other professional skills donated to support us.
