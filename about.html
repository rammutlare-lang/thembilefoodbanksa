Our Story

Why We Exist

Thembile FoodBank SA was founded by Ramalau Rammutla (Mr), a humanitarian driven by a deep commitment to human dignity, community upliftment and the fight against hunger.

The organisation was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to food, and no one should go to bed hungry while resources exist to help.

Under this vision, Thembile FoodBank SA works to fight hunger by producing, sourcing, purchasing, collecting, storing and distributing food and essential resources to communities experiencing food insecurity across South Africa. We bring together individuals, businesses, farmers, food producers, donors and strategic partners to transform available resources into meaningful support for those who need it most.

For Ramalau Rammutla, humanitarianism is not simply about providing food. It is about restoring hope, protecting dignity and creating opportunities for communities to become stronger and more food secure. His vision is to build an organisation that responds to immediate hunger while contributing to long-term solutions that address food insecurity at its roots.

Thembile FoodBank SA is committed to building a food-secure South Africa where resources are used responsibly, communities are empowered, and every person can access food with dignity.
