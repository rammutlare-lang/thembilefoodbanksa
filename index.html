About Us

Rescuing Food. Restoring Dignity.

Thembile FoodBank SA is committed to eliminating hunger and reducing food waste by rescuing surplus food and redistributing it to those who need it most.

Together, we can end hunger.

Rescue Food

We collect quality surplus food from businesses, farms and donors.

Feed People

We distribute food to families, shelters and community programmes.

Restore Hope

We empower communities and restore dignity through sustainable support.
