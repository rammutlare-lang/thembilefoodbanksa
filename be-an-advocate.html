Be An Advocate

Use your voice to raise awareness about hunger and the power of food rescue.

Spread The Word

Six ways to raise your voice for a hunger-free South Africa

Awareness moves people to act. Here's how you can help more people learn about Thembile FoodBank SA and get involved.

Share Thembile FoodBank SA

Tell your friends, family, colleagues and community about our mission. Word of mouth is one of our most powerful tools.

Download & Share Campaign Materials

Request posters, flyers and digital graphics to share at your workplace, school, church or community centre.

Follow Our Social Media

Follow, like and share our updates to help our stories and campaigns reach a wider audience.

Start A Food Drive

Organise a food drive at your school, workplace, church or community group and help us collect food for those in need.

Become A Community Advocate

Represent Thembile FoodBank SA in your community by speaking at local events, groups and gatherings.

Refer Potential Donors Or Partners

Know a business, organisation or individual who could support our mission? Introduce us — every connection helps.

Ready To Get Started?

  • Let us know how you'd like to help — as an individual, school, church, workplace or community group.
  • We'll share the resources, materials or guidance you need to make an impact.
  • Stay in touch — we love hearing about the food drives and campaigns our advocates run.

Get in touch and tell us how you'd like to advocate for Thembile FoodBank SA.
