Governance Principles

Six principles we operate by

Integrity

We act honestly and ethically.

Dignity

We serve people without humiliating or degrading them.

Accountability

We remain accountable for every resource entrusted to us.

Transparency

We communicate openly with donors, beneficiaries and stakeholders.

Efficiency

We seek maximum social impact from every rand and every kilogram of food.

Partnership

We believe hunger is best addressed through collaboration.
